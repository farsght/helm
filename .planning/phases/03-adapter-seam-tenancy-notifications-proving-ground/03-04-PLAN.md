| T-03-13 | Tampering | Optimistic enable-toggle with stale rollback | accept | TanStack rollback on error restores prior state; onSettled invalidates so next render is server-confirmed. Acceptable residual risk. |
| T-03-14 | Information Disclosure | Webhook URL logged or persisted | accept | URL is consumer-supplied and public (it's their own endpoint); no secret in the URL itself; displayed in UI but not sent to any analytics service. |
| T-03-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | accept | No new external packages. |
